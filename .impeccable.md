## Design Context

### Users
Chinese self-learners studying Spanish, primarily on mobile. They open the app for short daily sessions (5–30 words). They care about consistency, motivation, and visible progress. A broken study streak is emotionally charged — they need encouragement, not blame.

### Brand Personality
Warm, Spanish-flavored, encouraging, modern, personal, and a little playful.

### Aesthetic Direction
Glass-morphism with warm Spanish red and gold accents. Four switchable themes: warm glass (`caliente`), aurora, bright white (`luz`), and night (`nocturno`). Rounded cards, soft shadows, clear hierarchy. Avoid cold/neon/AI-default looks and punitive red alarms.

### Design Principles
1. **Encouragement over punishment** — especially for broken streaks and empty/error states.
2. **Strong text/background separation** on glass surfaces; no text should "melt" into the scene.
3. **Warm, saturated accents** (Spanish red/gold) that feel energetic rather than clinical.
4. **Mobile-first compact layouts** where key actions and status fit on one screen.
5. **Accessible contrast** across all four themes, including dark glass.
